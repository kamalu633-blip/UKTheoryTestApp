🛡️ Summary: We respect your privacy. Kamal's UK Driving Theory does not collect, track, or sell your personal data. All study progress, test scores, and bookmarks are stored 100% locally on your device.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Kamal Uddin ("Developer", "we", "our", or "us") handles information in relation to the mobile application Kamal's UK Driving Theory (the "App") available on iOS and Android platforms.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

The App is built with privacy-first principles:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address to use the App.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, addresses, phone numbers, or credit card details.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not track or request access to your GPS or physical location.
  • No Third-Party Ad Trackers: The App does not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

3. Local Device Data Storage

All user progress data is saved locally on your device via secure offline database storage (SQLite / AsyncStorage):

  • Category quiz completion and test history
  • Practice question bookmarks and flagged items
  • Hazard perception test scores and practice timestamps
  • Selected language preferences

This data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to any external servers.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

The App may include optional links to external services (such as contacting the developer via Email, WhatsApp, Phone, or LinkedIn, or viewing official DVSA guidelines). When you click an external link, you are subject to the privacy policy of that third-party service.

5. Compliance with UK GDPR and Data Protection Laws

The App is compliant with the UK Data Protection Act 2018 and the General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR). Because no personal data is collected or processed on remote servers, your statutory rights regarding data privacy are naturally preserved.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any personal data has inadvertently been provided, please contact us immediately so we can ensure it is deleted.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and accessible directly within the App.
